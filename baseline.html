Baseline Screener

A check that covers every Reading & Writing skill, so we know exactly what to work on first.

22 questions · 26 minutes.

Two questions on each of the eleven skills, all at the test's middle difficulty, at the real section's pace. You won't see whether an answer was right until the end — that's normal for a check like this, and it keeps the reading honest.

Afterwards there's a short optional follow-up that pins down how far your strongest skills go, and whether the weak ones need re-teaching or just practice.
